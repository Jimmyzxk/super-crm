# B2B Competitor Analysis & Sales Battlecard Generator

This skill enables deep competitive intelligence harvesting and actionable battlecard synthesis to help sales reps displace incumbent vendors and win head-to-head deals.

---

## 1. Competitive Dimensions to Research

1. **Product & Feature Parity Matrix**:
   - Core differentiators, exclusive strengths, and architectural gaps.
   - Usability, mobile experience, enterprise integration depth.
2. **Pricing & Commercial Terms**:
   - Pricing models (per user, per consumption, enterprise tiering).
   - Hidden costs (implementation fees, add-on modules, renewal price hikes).
3. **Customer Sentiment & Real User Reviews**:
   - Public review signals (G2, Capterra, Zhihu, V2EX, customer forums).
   - Most frequent customer complaints (downtime, slow support, complex UI).
4. **Objection Handling (话术攻防)**:
   - "Why us over [Competitor]?"
   - "How to respond when prospect says [Competitor] is cheaper?"

---

## 2. Standard Battlecard Structure

```markdown
### ⚔️ 竞品对抗卡: [Competitor Name] vs 我方方案

| 维度 | 我方方案 | [Competitor Name] | 胜出策略 (Winning Edge) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **产品架构** | 现代云原生 / 实时响应 | 传统单体 / 笨重 | 突出低门槛、敏捷与 AI 深度赋能 |
| **计费透明度** | 无隐形收费 | 实施费高 / 模块割裂 | 强调 TCO 综合拥有成本优势 |
| **服务响应** | 专属客户成功 / 极速响应 | 工单响应慢 | 强调本地化与深度陪伴交付 |

#### 🛡️ 核心异议攻防话术 (Trap Setting & Objection Handling)
1. **客户提及“竞品价格更低”**:
   - *响应话术*: ...
2. **客户提及“竞品功能很多”**:
   - *响应话术*: ...
```
